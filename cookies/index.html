Cookie notice

Version 0.1 (draft), 9 October 2026. Applies to the Egghead Labs company website at eggheadlabs.co.za.

The short version

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. There is nothing to accept or decline.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can save in your browser so it can recognise you later. Similar tools include local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting.

What this website uses

  • No cookies. Our pages do not set or read cookies.
  • No analytics. We do not count, profile or follow visitors, and no analytics service is installed.
  • No advertising or tracking. There are no advertising tags, tracking pixels, social media buttons or embedded content from other companies.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts. Our font is stored on our own site, so your browser does not contact a font service.
  • No browser storage. We do not use local storage or similar technologies.

Our hosting provider, GitHub, receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser asks for a page, as any web server does. That is a server log, not a cookie; see our privacy notice.

Links to other sites

If you follow a link to another website, that site's own cookie and privacy notices apply.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we will update this notice first, and we will ask for your consent before setting anything that is not strictly necessary for the site to work.

What you must check with counsel

  2. Whether GitHub Pages sets or reads any cookie or identifier on a custom domain (our own check of the live response headers found none; confirm this is the position in GitHub's terms).
  3. The consent wording to use if analytics are added later (POPIA and, for EU visitors, the ePrivacy rules).
