Privacy notice

Version 0.1 (draft), 9 October 2026. Applies to the Egghead Labs company website at eggheadlabs.co.za.

Egghead Labs is a small South African software company. This notice explains what personal information the company website handles, why, and what you can do about it. Each of our products has its own privacy notice, which applies when you use that product.

Who is responsible

Egghead Labs is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies. Contact us at support@eggheadlabs.co.za. Our registered name, registration number, postal address and the name of our Information Officer will be added here before this notice takes effect.

Personal information we collect

Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write in your message and any attachments.

Information collected automatically. The website itself collects nothing about you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no advertising or tracking code, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies. Our hosting provider, GitHub, receives the technical information every web server receives when your browser asks for a page, such as your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser type. GitHub uses this to deliver the site and keep it secure. See our cookie notice.

We do not buy personal information from anyone and we do not combine website visits with other information about you.

How we use your personal information

  • To answer your message and keep a record of the conversation.
  • To keep the website running and secure, through our hosting provider.
  • To meet legal obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information, send marketing you did not ask for, or make automated decisions about you from website visits.

Legal basis

Under POPIA we process your information because it is necessary to deal with your request, because it protects our legitimate interests in running a secure website and answering correspondence, or because the law requires it. The GDPR legal bases are set out in the section for people in the European Union below.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Google Workspace) provides our email. Messages you send us are stored in our mailbox there.
  • GitHub hosts the website and handles the server logs described above.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, where needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business or part of it is sold or reorganised, under the same protections.

International transfers

Google and GitHub may store and process information outside South Africa, including in the United States. We rely on the protections in their data processing terms. Under section 72 of POPIA we only transfer personal information outside South Africa where the recipient is bound by law, binding corporate rules or an agreement that gives protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where another ground in section 72 applies.

How long we keep it

We keep email correspondence for as long as we need it to deal with your request and for any follow-up, and then for up to five years in case of a legal question, unless the law requires longer. GitHub keeps its server logs under its own retention policy.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a security compromise affects your personal information we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

You may ask us whether we hold personal information about you and for a copy of it, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and withdraw any consent you gave. Email support@eggheadlabs.co.za. We will reply within a reasonable time and in any event within the period the law requires. We may ask you to confirm you control the email address involved.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

The company website is not aimed at children. If you are under 18 and write to us, we will only use your message to reply to you. Under POPIA, a child's personal information may generally be processed only with the consent of a competent person, such as a parent or guardian, unless another legal ground applies.

People in the European Union

If you are in the EU or the European Economic Area, the GDPR may apply when we handle your information.

  • Legal bases: answering your message is necessary to take steps at your request or is in our legitimate interest in responding (Article 6(1)(b) or (f)); server logs are kept in our legitimate interest in a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)); we keep records where the law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Your rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent where we rely on it.
  • Transfers: where information leaves the EEA we rely on adequacy decisions or the standard contractual clauses in our providers' terms.
  • Complaints: you may complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice we will update the version line at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell the people affected where we can.

Contact

Egghead Labs, South Africa. support@eggheadlabs.co.za

What you must check with counsel

  1. Registered name, registration number, physical address and the Information Officer's name (and registration with the Information Regulator) to be inserted before publication as final.
  2. Whether the GDPR applies at all to a site that only receives email, and whether an EU representative under Article 27 is needed.
  3. The five-year retention period for correspondence: is it right, or should it be shorter?
  4. Whether relying on Google's and GitHub's standard terms satisfies POPIA section 72 for transfers to the United States.
  5. Whether GitHub is our operator for its server logs or acts independently, and how to describe that.
